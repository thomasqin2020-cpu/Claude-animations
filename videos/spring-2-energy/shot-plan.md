## Fact basis (section 1.2 of the brief)

Work done stretching from 0 to x with applied force kx′ (quasi-static):

W = ∫₀ˣ kx′ dx′ = ½kx²

Stored as elastic potential energy: U = ½kx².

Work done by the spring from x₁ to x₂:

Wₛ = ∫ₓ₁ˣ² (−kx) dx = ½kx₁² − ½kx₂² = −ΔU

Video numbers (k = 10 N/m, x₁ = 0.20 m, x₂ = 0.40 m): Wₛ = ½(10)(0.04) − ½(10)(0.16) = −0.60 J.
U(0.20) = 0.20 J and U(0.40) = 0.80 J, so doubling x quadruples U (four-triangle tiling).
