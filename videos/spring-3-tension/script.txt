In an ideal massless spring, tension is equal everywhere.
A net force on a massless piece would mean infinite acceleration.
So a spring pulls equally hard on both ends.
Hang two springs in series, and each carries the full load, so the stretches add.
That gives one over k effective equals one over k one plus one over k two.
Side by side in parallel, they share one stretch, and their forces add.
Now k effective is just k one plus k two.
Series is softer than either spring.
Parallel is stiffer than both.
