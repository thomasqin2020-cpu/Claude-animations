## Recurring devices

- The pyramid (society.js createPyramid): sections 4, 6, 7, 8 and behind the farmers in section 11.

## Build notes

- Scenes are grouped by location family: openings.js, economy.js, society.js, bondage.js, resistance.js, politics.js; common.js holds the palette, lights, people and props.
- main.js builds the scene list from cue prefixes (T.K / T.KE). A prefix that matches zero or several lines throws at load.
- Render: node scripts/render.js --video ch16-slavery-long (about 0.5 s per frame; run in the background).
